# Workout Calendar

Personal workout log. Open it at https://orbelloelias-del.github.io/workout/

- `index.html` is the whole site.
- Your workouts are saved to your own Firebase database (project `workout-log-3db1e`) after you tap **Sign in with Google**. Each Google account has its own separate log; a new account starts empty.
- Use **Download backup** / **Import backup** on the site to export or restore your data as a JSON file.
